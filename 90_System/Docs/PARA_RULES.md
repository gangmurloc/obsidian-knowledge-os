# PARA Rules

## Projects

Projects are current efforts with a clear outcome and an explicit definition of done. A Project should make it possible to tell when the work is complete. When finished or abandoned, it moves to `40_Archives/Projects/` only after human approval.

Examples include publishing a paper, completing a course deliverable, or shipping a specific software release.

## Areas

Areas are responsibilities that require ongoing attention and have no planned end date. An Area is maintained through standards, reviews, and recurring work rather than completed once.

Examples include health, personal finance, research practice, and professional development. An inactive Area moves to `40_Archives/Areas/` only after human approval.

## Resources

Resources are reusable knowledge and reference material. External sources, AI-derived concepts, and human-owned knowledge remain separated under `30_Resources/` according to their ownership and provenance.

## Archives

Archives contain inactive Projects and Areas. Archiving preserves history and links; it is not deletion. Resources are not archived merely because a Project stops using them.

## Reference, Do Not Copy

> 지식 그 자체는 Projects 또는 Areas에 복사하지 않는다.

Project and Area notes reference relevant Resource notes through Wikilinks. They may record context, decisions, actions, and how knowledge is being applied, but they must not duplicate the canonical knowledge content.

```text
Project / Area --> [[Resource Note]]
```

This keeps one canonical knowledge note, preserves provenance, and prevents divergent copies.

## Classification Boundary

- Do not infer a user's Project, Area, Resource, or Archive classification automatically.
- Unclear captures stay in `00_Inbox/` until a person classifies them.
- Bulk moves, renames, and archive operations require an explicit preview and approval.
